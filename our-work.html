How we protect you from sub liensColorado law lets anyone who supplies labor or materials to your property file a lien if they aren't paid. We protect you from that — every sub is paid against a signed lien waiver, so a paid sub can't come back on your home.

Required disclosure (Colorado CRS §38-22-101): Any person or company supplying labor or materials for this improvement to your property may file a lien against your property if that person or company is not paid for the contributions.
