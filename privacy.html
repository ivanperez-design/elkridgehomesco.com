Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 24, 2026

This page explains what information Elk Ridge Home LLC collects when you contact us or use this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. The short version: we use your information to respond to your inquiry and run your project — we never sell it, and we never publish it.

Who we are

Elk Ridge Home LLC is an owner-run residential remodeling and construction company in Steamboat Springs, Colorado. You can reach us at info@elkridgeinteriors.com or 970-393-6239.

What we collect

  • Information you send us — your name, phone number, email, project address, project details, photos, and anything else you include when you submit a form on this website, submit a Facebook or Instagram lead form, or call, text, or email us.
  • Analytics data — basic usage information collected by Google Analytics cookies: pages visited, device and browser type, and approximate location. This data is aggregated and does not identify you by name.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your inquiry, prepare estimates and proposals, provide our services, and — with your consent — contact you by call, text, or email about your project and our services. We use analytics data to understand how the website is used and improve it.

Facebook and Instagram lead forms

If you submit one of our lead forms on Facebook or Instagram, Meta transmits the information you entered to us. From that point it is handled under this policy, the same as if you had submitted it on this website.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information, and we never publish or list your phone number anywhere. We share information only with service providers that help us run the business — for example form processing, analytics, scheduling, and e-signature services — and when the law requires it. Those providers may only use your information to provide their service to us.

Calls and text messages

If you consent to receive calls or texts from us: consent is never a condition of purchase; message frequency varies; message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to opt out — we honor opt-outs promptly, and always within ten business days — or reply HELP for help. You can also opt out any time by emailing or calling us.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the information we hold about you by emailing info@elkridgeinteriors.com or calling 970-393-6239. You can limit analytics cookies through your browser settings.

Legal requests

We disclose information when we are legally required to do so — for example in response to a valid subpoena or court order.

Audience

Our services and marketing are directed to adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we post the updated version on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your information: info@elkridgeinteriors.com · 970-393-6239 · Elk Ridge Home LLC, Steamboat Springs, CO.

How we protect you from sub liensColorado law lets anyone who supplies labor or materials to your property file a lien if they aren't paid. We protect you from that — every sub is paid against a signed lien waiver, so a paid sub can't come back on your home.

Required disclosure (Colorado CRS §38-22-101): Any person or company supplying labor or materials for this improvement to your property may file a lien against your property if that person or company is not paid for the contributions.
