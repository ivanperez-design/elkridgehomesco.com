Questions we get

Three things owners ask first.

Can we live in the home during a whole-home remodel?
Often, yes. We phase the work zone by zone and keep a usable kitchen and bathroom going where the scope allows, walling off active areas and protecting your finishes daily. It is slower than an empty house and there will be stretches without a kitchen, so we lay that out honestly at the walkthrough.
We live out of town. How does that work?
It is built for you. You get a photo and status update every week, a written weekly recap, selections and change orders handled by phone, text, and email, and one owner as your single point of contact. Most out-of-town owners see the house in person at the end, not in the middle.
Do you take on additions and second stories, or only interior work?
Both. We take on a home addition as part of a remodel or on its own — new foundation to frost depth, matched roofline and siding so it reads as one home, permits and setbacks checked against your parcel before design gets too far along.
