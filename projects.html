Case studies

Full case studies, published as jobs close

We're early enough in Elk Ridge that we'd rather show you nothing than show you something invented. As each project finishes with the homeowner's consent, we publish it here as a full case study — the real story of the job, start to finish, with owned before-and-after photography. We won't post a project name, address, or client name without permission, and we won't fabricate one to fill space.

This page is for full case studies — one finished project told start to finish. For the running gallery of finished craftsmanship from our crew, see Our Work.

Here's exactly what each published case study will walk through:

  1. 01

    The Problem

    What the homeowner was up against — the failing deck, the cold drafty windows, the kitchen that never worked, or the bare lot waiting on a custom build.

  2. 02

    The Scope

    The written, line-item plan we signed before demo: what was getting built, the allowances, the timeline, and the Steamboat-specific details the job demanded — snow load, frost-depth footings, Climate Zone 7B insulation, WUI-rated assemblies where the parcel required them.

  3. 03

    The Build

    How it actually went — the daily photo updates, the change orders approved in writing, the problems we found once we opened things up, and how we solved them without surprise invoices.

  4. 04

    The Result

    The finished work, the final walkthrough, and the written 2-year workmanship warranty that started that day — in the homeowner's own words once we have their consent.

How we protect you from sub liensColorado law lets anyone who supplies labor or materials to your property file a lien if they aren't paid. We protect you from that — every sub is paid against a signed lien waiver, so a paid sub can't come back on your home.

Required disclosure (Colorado CRS §38-22-101): Any person or company supplying labor or materials for this improvement to your property may file a lien against your property if that person or company is not paid for the contributions.
