The Elk Ridge Promise

A custom build or remodel is a lot of money and a lot of trust. So we put the things that usually go wrong in writing, up front, as promises you can hold us to.

  1. 01

    The 30-Minute Promise

    Call or message during business hours and you hear back within 30 minutes. You never chase us.

  2. 02

    The No-Surprise Scope Guarantee

    A written, line-item scope, signed before demo day. The price changes only by a change order you approve in writing — never a surprise invoice.

  3. 03

    The Daily Visibility Guarantee

    A photo and status update every working day, plus a short written weekly recap, sent to whoever you want, wherever you are.

  4. 04

    The Broom-Clean Guarantee

    Your home protected and broom-clean at the end of every working day, with a professional deep-clean at completion.

  5. 05

    The Verified-Crew Guarantee

    Every sub on your job has a current insurance certificate, Colorado trade license where applicable, and signed agreement on file before they set foot in your home.

Plus: a written 2-year workmanship warranty — above the local 1-year norm — and final payment only after you sign off at the satisfaction walkthrough.

Good questions

Questions homeowners ask us

Are you licensed and insured?
Yes — Elk Ridge Home is local, owner-run, licensed and insured in Colorado, and we're glad to send our certificate of insurance on request. Every subcontractor on your job also carries a current insurance certificate, Colorado trade license where applicable, and a signed agreement before they start.
Do you handle design, or do I need to bring my own designer?
Both work. We lead scope and layout direction as part of your walkthrough, and we can guide selections with a documented allowance schedule. If you already have a designer or architect, we coordinate with them directly.
What's the smallest project you take on?
We focus on custom home builds, whole-home and major remodels, and full-room interior and exterior projects — new builds, kitchens, baths, basements, whole-home interiors, decks, siding, framing, and windows — rather than small repairs or single-item handyman work. If you're not sure where your project lands, Text Us a few photos and we'll tell you honestly.
How will I know what's happening on my project?
You get a photo and a status update every working day, plus a short written weekly recap, sent to whoever you choose. And you reach us directly — not a foreman, not a call center — the same business day.
How does the budget stay in control without surprise costs?
We sign a written, line-item scope before demo day. From there, the price changes only by a change order you approve in writing — never a surprise invoice. What drives the final number is mostly scope, finish level, and the condition behind the walls; we walk through all of that with you before you commit.
Do you work with out-of-town and second-home owners?
Often. We start remote owners with a phone call and a photo-and-video walkthrough, run the job with daily photo updates and a written weekly recap, and keep one accountable owner on it the whole way, so you don't have to be in Steamboat to stay in the loop.
What does the 2-year workmanship warranty cover, and when can you start?
Our written 2-year workmanship warranty covers the quality of our work and starts the day of your final walkthrough — above the local 1-year norm. Start dates depend on the season and current schedule; mountain trades and materials book out weeks ahead in summer, so the sooner we do your walkthrough, the better your window.

How we protect you from sub liensColorado law lets anyone who supplies labor or materials to your property file a lien if they aren't paid. We protect you from that — every sub is paid against a signed lien waiver, so a paid sub can't come back on your home.

Required disclosure (Colorado CRS §38-22-101): Any person or company supplying labor or materials for this improvement to your property may file a lien against your property if that person or company is not paid for the contributions.
