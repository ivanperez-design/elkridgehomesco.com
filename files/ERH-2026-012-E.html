SITUATION

The loading-dock overhead door at the Hayden Post Office has sustained impact damage: the upper glazing panel is shattered, the door will not close, and the door frame and adjacent siding/trim are damaged. The building is open to weather and unsecured. Client has requested immediate after-hours emergency securement.

SCOPE OF WORK — PHASE 1: EMERGENCY SECUREMENT (TONIGHT)

  • Remove and safely dispose of the shattered glazing panel and all loose glass/debris at the dock opening.
  • Secure or remove damaged door sections as required to make the opening safe.
  • Frame and sheath the full dock-door opening with structural plywood/OSB over lumber framing — weather-tight and tamper-resistant, fastened to sound structure.
  • Protect interior contents and equipment during the work; broom-clean the work area.
  • Full photo documentation (before / during / after) provided with the invoice.

Phase 2 — Permanent Repair: replacement of the overhead door, glazing, frame, and damaged siding/trim will be assessed on site tonight and quoted under a separate proposal. No permanent-repair work is included in this authorization.
