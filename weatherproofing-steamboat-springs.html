Water, managed as a system

What goes into weatherproofing your home

Here is what weatherproofing actually involves, what moves the budget, and how we run it — in plain English. Water is patient: it finds the one detail nobody sealed, and a mountain freeze-thaw cycle widens that gap every single winter. You will leave this page knowing what protects a home up here, without the jargon.

Flashing details

The metal and membrane that direct water away at every seam: windows, doors, rooflines, decks, and where two materials meet. Most leaks are a flashing detail done wrong, not a product that failed.

Drainage & grading

The ground should slope away from the house, and water should have somewhere to go. We grade and route it so snowmelt doesn't pool against the foundation.

Foundation drainage

Perimeter drains and proper backfill keep groundwater and spring melt out of basements and crawlspaces.

Air & envelope sealing

Sealing the gaps where heated air escapes and cold air (and moisture) sneaks in. It lowers your heat bill and stops condensation problems before they start.

Overall water management

Gutters, downspouts, and the path water takes off the roof and away from the structure, planned as one system rather than patched piece by piece.

Weatherproofing is the envelope layer of every exterior remodel we do — or Request a Consultation and we'll look at yours.

Straight talk on budget

What drives the cost — and how we keep you in control

Most of a weatherproofing number comes down to scope (one problem area vs. the whole envelope), access, and what we find once we open a wall or pull back trim. We walk all of it with you, put it in a written line-item scope, and the price changes only by a change order you approve.

Good questions

Questions homeowners ask us

My leak shows up inside — can you find the source?
Yes. Water travels, so the stain is rarely above the failure. We trace it back to the real entry point before we repair, so it doesn't come back.
Can you fix this without a full exterior remodel?
Often. Many envelope problems are targeted flashing or drainage fixes. We scope it in writing either way and tell you honestly if it's a repair or a bigger job.
Will sealing the house cause moisture problems?
Done right, the opposite — we balance sealing with ventilation so the home stays dry and healthy.
What does the warranty cover?
A written 2-year workmanship warranty on our work, starting at your final walkthrough.
