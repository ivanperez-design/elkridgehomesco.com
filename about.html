Where we come from

A focused remodeler, built for mountain homes.

Elk Ridge Home is a new company with an experienced builder behind it. We're based in Steamboat Springs and we work across Routt County — Hayden, Craig, Oak Creek, Stagecoach, Clark, and the wider Yampa Valley. We remodel the homes that already mean something to the people who own them — kitchens, bathrooms, basements, and whole-home interiors, plus decks, siding, roofing, and windows.

We started Elk Ridge because too many remodels in this valley run the same way — a handshake, a vague number, a crew you never see the same face on twice, and a final bill that doesn't match the conversation you thought you had. We built this company to do the opposite. A written scope you approve before demo. A real person who answers the phone. A photo at the end of every working day. The result is a remodel that feels handled, not hoped for.

How we protect your home and your peace of mind

The No-Surprise Steamboat Remodel.

Most remodel horror stories aren't really about bad work — they're about silence. Weeks with no update. A price that moved without a conversation. A crew that left the house a mess. We treat communication as part of the craft, not an afterthought to it.

Good craftsmanship and good communication aren't separate things to us — they're the same standard, applied to the work and to the way we keep you in the loop. A remodel is one of the larger investments you'll make in your home, and it happens inside the place you live. You deserve to know what's happening in it, every day.

So we run it in the open:

A written scope before demo day — line by line, approved by you before a nail goes in. The price changes only by a change order you sign — never a surprise invoice.
A photo and status update every working day — sent to whoever you want, wherever you are, plus a short written weekly recap.
Your home protected and broom-clean at the end of every working day, with a professional deep-clean at completion.
Final payment only after you sign off at the satisfaction walkthrough.

None of this is a perk. It's the floor we built the company on — the antidote to the remodel everyone warns you about.

Why local matters here

Remodels built for the way mountain homes actually live.

Steamboat isn't a flatland suburb, and remodeling here isn't the same job it is at sea level. Homes at this altitude move with deep freeze-thaw cycles, carry real snow load, and sit in a climate that punishes shortcuts. Materials take longer to reach us — remote mountain delivery adds days to most orders — and the trades book out weeks ahead in the summer season. A remodeler who understands that plans around it instead of getting caught by it.

We're local, and we build for local conditions and local code. We work for the people who live here year-round and for second-home owners who manage their property from out of state — and the same standard holds either way. If you're remote, you start with a phone call and a photo-and-video walkthrough, and you get the same daily updates as the owner who lives three blocks from the jobsite. Distance changes the logistics, never the standard.

What we build

Custom homes and remodels, built to the same standard.

Elk Ridge builds new custom homes and remodels existing ones — and the discipline behind both is the same. A written scope you approve before work begins, the owner on site, daily photos, a clean house every night. Whether we're framing a new home from the foundation up or reworking the one you already live in, the work is run the same way, with the same person accountable from first call to final walkthrough.

That range is deliberate. A custom build and a deep remodel draw on the same craft — structure, envelope, mechanicals, finish — and on the same Steamboat-specific knowledge: snow load, 48-inch frost depth, Climate Zone 7B continuous exterior insulation, triple-pane windows, WUI wildfire detailing, and material lead times that punish anyone who plans like they're at sea level. We build for those conditions on every job, new or existing.

If you're planning a new home, a major addition, or a remodel of any size, you're in the right place. We'll walk it with you, tell you honestly what it involves, and put it in writing.
